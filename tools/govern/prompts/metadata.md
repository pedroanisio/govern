Rules (PUBLISHING.md):
- Title: a claim or a question someone would want answered, sentence case. A Short's title says the thing plainly and usually carries the key number. No #shorts, no episode numbers, no channel name. Max 100 characters.
- Description, in this order: a first line that works alone; two or three sentences of explanation including any condition that changes a claim's meaning; one ask at most; a line starting `Source:` with the primary link(s) taken from the material (never invent a URL; if none is given write `Source: (owner to add)`); `Figures checked <date>.` when figures are given; disclose generated or conceptual artwork.

- Up to three hashtags.
- Nothing from production: no file names, scene names, ".xml", ".scene", "TODO".
- The title and first line promise only what the first 30 s deliver.
- pinned_comment: the source comment (primary sources, one line each); for a Short also name the long film it comes from if the brief says "from".
